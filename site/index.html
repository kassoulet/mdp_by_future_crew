MDP, MusicDiscPlayer

Future Crew's S3M/MOD player and music disk engine (1993), recreated for the browser at its native 640x480.

Journey IPurple Motion, 7 tunes, MDP v1.0 Journey IIPurple Motion, 7 tunes, MDP v1.0 Chaotic MindSkaven, 8 tunes, MDP v1.1

How it was reversed and ported

Mouse: wheel scrolls, click selects, click again plays, click the bar to seek. Keys: arrows, PgUp/PgDn, Enter, Esc, Backspace for back. All music and software belong to their authors.
